From Waste to Wonder

Founded by Alicia Barrie-George, Glastic Designs (a play on glass, plants and plastics) began with a vision to introduce green spaces into urban Trinidad. After recognizing the massive volume of plastic waste, we pivoted to upcycling — turning discarded materials into high-value interior products.

Circular Economy

Every product gives plastic a second life, reducing landfill waste and pollution in our communities and oceans.

School Furniture

We refurbish discarded school furniture using recycled plastic, providing low-cost solutions and better learning environments.

How We Make It

1

Collect

We collect plastic waste at events, from partners and communities across Trinidad and Tobago.

2

Sort & Shred

Plastics are sorted by type and color, then shredded for consistent material quality.

3

Process

Using Precious Plastic techniques, we melt and form the plastic into sheets, tiles and components.

4

Create

Crafted into durable, beautiful products ready for homes, schools and businesses.

Making a Difference

  • Collected tens of kilograms at events (e.g. 33 lbs at Honeyfest, 60kg with partners)
  • Partnered with NGC and others supporting local recycling efforts
  • Changing the cultural perspective: plastic is a resource, not trash
  • Supporting Every Bottle Back TT and circular economy initiatives
